About i3 International

AI video for loss prevention and operations.

For more than 35 years, i3 International has helped businesses prevent loss, improve operations and act faster on real-time data. In grocery, it works with the cameras and POS systems you already have.

In one year, i3 helped a 27-store regional grocer surface more than $370,000 in preventable losses.
